## Correct interpretation
OpenRouter's final review examines the execution trace metadata only. It is not a substitute for verifying the resulting state in the connected SaaS application or for functional/security acceptance testing. A workflow marked `COMPLETED` means the planned actions have success records and the configured model returned a `PASS` on the trace; it does not mean PBE has replaced that SaaS product.
